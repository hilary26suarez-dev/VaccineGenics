# HLA-Vaccine Immunogenicity Literature

## Overview
The HLA system is the primary genetic determinant of adaptive immune response to vaccines.
With >35,000 documented alleles across HLA-A, -B, -C, -DRB1, -DQB1, -DPB1 loci,
individual variation in antigen presentation is immense.

## Key Studies

### COVID-19 Vaccine HLA Associations
1. **Iturrieta-Zuazo et al., 2021** (PMID: 34129572)
   - Cohort: 1,638 healthcare workers, BNT162b2 vaccine
   - Finding: HLA-A*02:01 carriers had 1.8× higher spike-specific CD8+ T-cell response
   - HLA-DRB1*03:01 associated with highest IgG titers (OR 2.4)
   - HLA-DRB1*01:01 associated with lowest titers

2. **Augusto et al., 2023** — Overview HLA variants in COVID-19 vaccine-induced autoimmunity
   - HLA-DRB1*11:04: VITT risk (adenoviral vector vaccines)
   - HLA-B*35: Post-vaccine myocarditis and thyroiditis risk
   - HLA-C*04: Subacute thyroiditis post-vaccination
   - Source: ijmeg0167776.pdf

### Influenza Vaccine HLA Studies
- HLA-DQB1*06:02 linked to higher hemagglutination inhibition titers (PMID: 16227993)
- HLA-B*08:01 associated with cross-reactive T-cell response to influenza A strains

### Hepatitis B Vaccine Non-responders
- 5-10% of vaccinated individuals fail to seroconvert
- HLA-DRB1*07:01 and HLA-DQB1*02:02 haplotype: non-responder phenotype (PMID: 8552948)
- Mechanism: Poor HBsAg peptide binding to class II groove

## VITT (Vaccine-Induced Immune Thrombocytopenia and Thrombosis)

### Mechanism
1. Adenoviral vector vaccine → anti-PF4 (platelet factor 4) IgG antibody formation
2. Anti-PF4 antibodies bind platelet FcγRIIa receptors → platelet activation
3. Thrombosis + thrombocytopenia within 4-28 days post-vaccination

### HLA Association
- **HLA-DRB1*11:04** significantly overrepresented in VITT cases
- Proposed mechanism: specific HLA groove presents PF4-derived peptides → breaks tolerance
- PMID: 34255042 (Pavord et al., NEJM 2021)
- PMID: 33913550 (Greinacher et al., NEJM 2021)

### Clinical Criteria
- Thrombocytopenia (platelets <100,000/μL)
- Thrombosis (venous or arterial, especially cerebral venous sinus)
- Positive anti-PF4 antibody ELISA
- Onset: 4-28 days post ChAdOx1 or Ad26.COV2.S vaccination

## Heredity of Vaccine Response

### Twin Studies
- Anti-measles antibody titers: heritability 88.5% (PMID: 19234521)
- Anti-rubella titers: heritability 46% 
- Anti-mumps titers: heritability 39%
- Implication: genetic testing can predict vaccine response with high confidence

### Genome-Wide Association Studies (GWAS)
- GWAS for HBV vaccine response: 6 significant loci, HLA explains 40% of variance
- GWAS for influenza vaccine: HLA-DQA1 top hit (PMID: 22307276)
- GWAS for COVID-19 vaccine NAb titers: HLA-A, IGHV genes, complement genes (PMC9748098)
